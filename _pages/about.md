  <p>I am a Ph.D. student in the <a href="https://www.me.utexas.edu/">Walker Department of Mechanical Engineering</a> at UT Austin, co-advised by <a href="http://users.oden.utexas.edu/~omar">Prof. Omar Ghattas</a> and <a href="https://jacobsschool.ucsd.edu/people/profile/mehran-tehrani">Prof. Mehran Tehrani</a>, and affiliated with the <a href="https://oden.utexas.edu">Oden Institute for Computational Engineering and Sciences</a>.   <div class="home-section__heading">
    <p class="home-kicker">Research focus</p>
    <h2 id="home-work-title">Mechanics-informed tools for composite manufacturing.</h2>

  <div class="home-pathways">
    <a class="home-pathway" href="/composites/">

      <strong>Thermoplastic composites</strong>

    </a>

<section class="home-labtrail" aria-labelledby="home-method-title">
  <div>
    <p class="home-kicker">Method</p>
    <h2 id="home-method-title">Experiments, models, and inference in the same loop.</h2>
    <p>I work on problems where material behavior, manufacturing constraints, and numerical methods have to be treated together. The goal is practical: build models that are physically interpretable, computationally scalable, and useful in real processing environments.</p>
  </div>
</section>
