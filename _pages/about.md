<section class="home-about" aria-labelledby="home-about-title">
  <div class="home-section__heading">
    <p class="home-kicker">About</p>
    <h2 id="home-about-title">Making manufacturing measurable, modelable, and controllable.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="home-about__body">
    <p>I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at <a href="https://www.nasa.gov/langley/">NASA Langley Research Center</a>, where I work with Tyler Hudson on <a href="https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240013257">OATMEAL</a> and energy-efficient thermoplastic composite processing. In Fall 2027, I will join the <a href="https://www.mech.utah.edu/">University of Utah Department of Mechanical Engineering</a> as an assistant professor.</p>
    <p>At Utah, I will launch a research group focused on digitally integrated, energy-efficient manufacturing of high-performance structures, including settings where sensing, automation, and trustworthy models must support decisions without constant human oversight. I completed my Ph.D. at UT Austin, co-advised by <a href="http://users.oden.utexas.edu/~omar">Prof. Omar Ghattas</a> and <a href="https://jacobsschool.ucsd.edu/people/profile/mehran-tehrani">Prof. Mehran Tehrani</a>.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="home-section" aria-labelledby="home-work-title">
  <div class="home-section__heading">
    <p class="home-kicker">Research program</p>
    <h2 id="home-work-title">Three thrusts for the future Utah research group.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="home-pathways">
    <a class="home-pathway" href="/composites/">
      <span class="home-pathway__label">01 / intelligent materials</span>
      <strong>Sensor-integrated, energy-efficient composites</strong>
      <p>Extending OATMEAL toward thermoplastic material systems designed for high-rate processing, embedded sensing, and closed-loop manufacturing.</p>
    </a>
    <a class="home-pathway" href="/inverse-problems/">
      <span class="home-pathway__label">02 / real-time inference</span>
      <strong>Surrogate-enabled inverse frameworks</strong>
      <p>Recovering spatially varying manufacturing state from indirect observations using PDE-constrained optimization, neural operators, reduced models, and uncertainty-aware inference.</p>
    </a>
    <a class="home-pathway" href="/publications/">
      <span class="home-pathway__label">03 / resilient autonomy</span>
      <strong>Manufacturing autonomy for remote environments</strong>
      <p>Closing the loop between process models, sensing, inversion, and control so composite manufacturing can adapt in operator-sparse settings, including space and other remote aerospace environments.</p>
    </a>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="home-labtrail" aria-labelledby="home-method-title">
  <div>
    <p class="home-kicker">Approach</p>
    <h2 id="home-method-title">Manufacturing physics, sensing, and autonomy in the same loop.</h2>
    <p>The group I am building at Utah will connect material processing experiments, in-situ sensing, thermal and mechanical characterization, and computation for high-dimensional inverse problems, surrogate modeling, optimization, and uncertainty quantification.</p>
  </div>
</section>
